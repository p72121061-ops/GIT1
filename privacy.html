Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Vrindavan Tutorials ("we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting the privacy of our students and their families. This policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and how it is used, in accordance with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act).

1. Information We Collect

  • Student name, class, and contact details
  • Parent/guardian name and contact number
  • Fee and payment records
  • Attendance and academic performance data
  • Technical data such as browser type, used only to improve site performance

2. Why We Collect It

We use this information solely to manage enrollment, track fee payments, communicate updates about classes, and provide academic support. We do not use your data for any purpose beyond running the tutorial services.

3. Consent for Minors

Most of our students are minors. In compliance with the DPDP Act, 2023, we collect and process a minor's personal data only with verifiable consent from a parent or legal guardian, obtained at the time of enrollment.

4. Photo & Video Consent

From time to time, we may photograph or record students during classes, events, or achievements for use on our website, social media, or promotional material. We do so only with the consent of a parent or guardian, obtained at the time of enrollment or separately when required. You may withdraw this consent at any time by contacting us, and we will remove the relevant photo or video within a reasonable time.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal data to third parties. Data may be shared only with service providers essential to running the platform (for example, our chat support tool), and only to the extent necessary.

6. Cookies & Local Storage

Our website may use cookies or browser local storage to remember preferences and improve your experience. No tracking data is sold or shared with advertisers.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable technical measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, alteration, or loss.

8. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us using the details below.

9. Data Retention

We retain student and fee records for as long as the student is enrolled, and for a reasonable period afterward for record-keeping and legal purposes. You may request earlier deletion of your data, subject to any legal or academic record-keeping requirements.
